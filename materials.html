Technical Material Procurement Guidelines

Selecting Centering Plate Quantities

To accurately estimate steel centering plates on rent, calculate your total slab area in square feet and divide by the plate size area (6 sq. ft for 3 ft × 2 ft plates, and 8 sq. ft for 4 ft × 2 ft plates). Add a standard 5% layout border overlap margin to account for variable column edges. Compare formwork methods in our shuttering vs shoring guide.

Cuplock Spacing & Structural Load Rules

Vertical Cuplock spacing, ledger lift intervals, and diagonal bracing must always be determined by a qualified structural engineer based on slab load, height, and project conditions. Explore key system advantages in our Cuplock scaffolding technical guide and verify site protocols using our safety checklist.
