Technical Material Procurement Guidelines

Selecting Centering Plate Quantities

To accurately estimate steel centering plates on rent, calculate your total slab area in square feet and divide by the plate size area (6 sq. ft for 3x2 plates, and 8 sq. ft for 4x2 plates). Add a standard 5% layout border overlap margin to account for variable column edges. Compare formwork methods in our shuttering vs shoring guide.

Cuplock Spacing & Structural Load Rules

Under standard civil engineering practices, vertical cuplock standards should be spaced 1.2 m to 1.5 m apart for slab heights up to 4 m, subject to site engineer calculations. Explore key system advantages in our Cuplock scaffolding technical guide and verify site protocols using our safety checklist.
