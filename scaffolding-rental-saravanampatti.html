Formwork & Centering

Shuttering Equipment Overview for Saravanampatti

Consistent slab finishing and multi-floor column alignment across Saravanampatti developments depend on well-matched shuttering hardware and accessories:

Shuttering Plates

Mild steel plates in 3 ft × 2 ft and 4 ft × 2 ft sizes with perimeter angles for level slab casting.

Prop Jacks

Telescopic steel props in 2.0 m–3.5 m and 2.5 m–4.0 m ranges for vertical slab shoring.

MS Channels

Rigid mild steel beam runners supporting soffit plates and concrete floor beam alignments.

MS Challi

Anti-slip 6 ft steel walkway planks bridging ledger grids for elevated trade access.

Wooden Balli

Selected eucalyptus poles for secondary diagonal bracing and light formwork support.

Wooden Fatta

Hardwood planks for beam bottom packing, perimeter closures, and infill strips.

Farma Plates

Modular steel column molds with pin-wedges for casting square and rectangular columns.

Acrow Spans

Adjustable horizontal trusses (2.4 m to 4.0 m) carrying centering plates over wide bays.

Base Jack & U Jack

Standards / Verticals & Ledgers / Horizontals

Cuplock verticals (1.0 m–3.0 m) locked to horizontals (850 mm–1950 mm).

Joint Spigot Pins & Couplers

Project Economics

Benefits of Organised Equipment Rental Planning

Structured formwork procurement supports cashflow and cleaner stage transitions across Saravanampatti schedules.

1. Aligning Equipment Volume with Staggered Tower Schedules

Residential and campus projects rarely pour every block simultaneously. Planning rental enquiries around phased tower cycles avoids paying to store idle shuttering plates on congested site perimeters while keeping active pours supplied.

2. Predictable Dimensional Consistency Across Repeat Floors

Multi-storey apartments and hostel wings repeat identical bay dimensions across five to twelve levels. Using uniform steel plates, Column Farma, and Acrow Spans keeps concrete soffits uniform and minimises finishing rework.

3. Reduced Maintenance & Yard Logistics Burden

Storing corroded pipes or re-threading damaged jacks eats into contractor margins between builds. Organised rental coordination places heavy storage and pre-dispatch inspection workflows outside your site team’s direct overhead.
