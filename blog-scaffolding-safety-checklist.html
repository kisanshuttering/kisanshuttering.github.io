Scaffolding Safety Checklist for Multi-Storey Construction Sites

Published by: Kisan Shuttering Editorial Team

Consistent site inspection routines help contractors, safety officers, and site engineers identify worn components or alignment issues before facade work or slab casting begins. Use this structured field checklist alongside our rental material specifications during daily shift handovers.

1. Foundation Preparation and Base Jack Seating

Scaffolding should rest on firm, prepared ground rather than loose soil or uneven fill. Checking base supports before erecting vertical standards prevents uneven settlement across bays:

Ground Leveling and Base Hardware

  • Establish Level Ground: Compact and level the ground surface before laying out the base grid.
  • Distribute Loads via Sole Plates: Position timber sole boards or spreader plates beneath all adjustable base jacks where soil or paving requires load distribution.
  • Thread Jack Thresholds: Keep adjustable base jack thread extensions within your project engineer's approved limits and verify collar nuts sit cleanly.

2. Vertical Alignment and Ledger Leveling

Small alignment errors at lower lifts compound as additional levels are erected. Checking vertical plumbness and horizontal levels early keeps the entire grid consistent:

Plumb and Level Verification

  • Vertical Standards Plumb Check: Confirm vertical standard plumbness with a spirit level or transit check at each lift height.
  • Ledger Horizontals Leveling: Verify that horizontal ledgers sit level and locked inside their node points across all bays.

3. Bracing Patterns and Coupler Checks

Bracing and couplers connect individual bays into a unified scaffold assembly under your site engineering plan. Verify hardware placement before authorizing platform access:

Diagonal, Plan, and Clamp Checks

  • Diagonal Face Bracing: Confirm diagonal braces are fitted across outer scaffold faces in accordance with your site layout.
  • Plan Bracing: Verify plan braces are installed at designated intervals as required by the project scaffold drawing.
  • Node Point Clamping: Check that right-angle and swivel clamps are seated cleanly near ledger-standard intersections with undamaged threads.

4. Working Platform & MS Challi Walkboard Securing

Working decks require even, slip-resistant boarding and perimeter edge protection before crews carry tools or materials overhead:

Decking and Edge Protection Checks

  • Full Decking: Place MS Challi walkboards side by side across the bay so no unintended gaps remain on the working platform.
  • Plank Securing: Fasten walkboard ends securely using tie wires or locking clips so boards cannot shift or lift in wind.
  • Guardrail Systems: Confirm toe-boards, mid-rails, and top guardrails are fitted along open platform edges.

5. Daily Inspection & Regional Material Support

Ongoing daily checks keep components accounted for throughout the rental cycle. Implement a daily tagging protocol to review weld joints, check thread condition, and confirm base jack seating. A clear inspection routine—from compacted ground and base jacks to plumbed standards, secured bracing, and tied-down MS Challi platforms—helps site teams catch damaged or loose parts early.
