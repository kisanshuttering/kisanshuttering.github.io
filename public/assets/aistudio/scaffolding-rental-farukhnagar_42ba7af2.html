CIVIL ENGINEERING GUIDE

Material Planning & Safety Guidelines

Essential considerations for planning material loading, vertical support distribution, and scaffolding alignments at your construction site.

1

Slab Load & Support Calculation

Always support wet concrete with adequate prop jacks. Prop spacing, grid pattern, and bracing must be determined by a qualified structural engineer based on slab thickness, dead and live loads, and formwork specifications.

2

Cuplock Scaffolding Alignment

Follow your structural engineer's spacing and bracing design, plumb standards on sole plates or compacted ground, and securely lock horizontal ledgers into node cups.

3

Pre-Pour Civil Inspection Checklist

Verify cuplock node tightness, pin-locks, prop plumbness, and MS challi walkboard connections before starting casting pour series.
