Types of Scaffolding Equipment Evaluated in Bangalore

Cuplock Scaffolding

Modular steel Cuplock connects Standards (1.0 m–3.0 m) and Ledgers (850 mm–1950 mm) at fixed node cups for slab shoring and exterior access.

Aluminium Scaffolding

Lightweight Aluminium Scaffolding suits facility maintenance and quick vertical access; availability for Bangalore is confirmed upon enquiry.

Pipe Scaffolding

Mobile Scaffolding

Rolling Scaffolding

Indoor Scaffolding

Formwork & Centering

Shuttering Equipment Overview for Bangalore

Concrete casting cycles across Bangalore podiums, core towers, and industrial aprons depend on verified mild steel and timber shuttering accessories:

Shuttering Plates

Mild steel plates in 3 ft × 2 ft and 4 ft × 2 ft sizes with perimeter angles for level slab casting.

Prop Jacks

Telescopic steel props in 2.0 m–3.5 m and 2.5 m–4.0 m ranges for vertical slab shoring.

MS Channels

Rigid mild steel beam runners supporting soffit plates and concrete floor beam alignments.

MS Challi

Anti-slip 6 ft steel walkway planks bridging ledger grids for elevated trade access.

Wooden Balli

Selected eucalyptus poles for secondary diagonal bracing and light formwork support.

Wooden Fatta

Hardwood planks for beam bottom packing, perimeter closures, and infill strips.

Farma Plates

Modular steel column molds with pin-wedges for casting square and rectangular columns.

Acrow Spans

Adjustable horizontal trusses (2.4 m to 4.0 m) carrying centering plates over wide bays.

Essential Scaffolding & Shuttering Accessories

Base Jack & U Jack

Screw levelers (350 mm–450 mm stem) under Base and atop Standards for beam runners.

Standards / Verticals & Ledgers / Horizontals

Cuplock verticals (1.0 m–3.0 m) locked to horizontals (850 mm–1950 mm).

Joint Spigot Pins & Couplers

Walkway Challi

Factors to Consider Before Selecting Equipment

Validate final load calculations, prop spacing, and bracing with your site’s structural engineer before erection.

1

Traffic Restrictions & Night Unloading Logistics

Heavy commercial vehicles face daytime restrictions across key Bengaluru arterial roads. Factor unloading slots, crane availability, and flatbed staging into your availability enquiry.

2

Monsoon Ground Preparation & Leveling

Bengaluru red-soil excavations soften quickly during peak rain spells; pair Base Jacks (350 mm–450 mm) with proper sole plates on compacted ground as specified by your structural engineer.

Slab Thickness vs Shoring Selection
