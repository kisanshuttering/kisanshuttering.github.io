PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Equipment Description & Yard Checks

Farma Plates are fabricated mild steel vertical formwork molds equipped with reinforced perimeter angles and slotted locking flanges. Designed to clamp around rebar cages and resist outward concrete pressure during pouring and vibration, they help deliver crisp column corners without timber warping.
